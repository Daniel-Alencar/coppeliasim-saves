Exercise: Potential Field Robot Navigation
In this exercise, you will build a with CoppeliaSim, a script to control a differential-drive robot using the Potential Field algorithm.

Objective
The robot must autonomously navigate in the environment, moving toward bananas while avoiding poops. The robot's motion must be generated by the potential field and not by a predefined trajectory or a sequence of waypoints.

Map information
The map is already generated for you. You do not need to generate or detect the bananas and poops.

Their positions are provided through two CoppeliaSim signals:

banana — positions of the bananas
poop — positions of the poops
The signals contain packed Lua tables. You can read them using:

local bananaSignal = sim.getStringSignal('banana')
local poopSignal = sim.getStringSignal('poop')

local bananas = sim.unpackTable(bananaSignal)
local poops = sim.unpackTable(poopSignal)
The positions are stored as pairs of coordinates:

[x1, y1, x2, y2, x3, y3, ...]
Your task
Build a controler for myRobot that implements the potential-field navigation algorithm.

The controller should:

Read the banana and poop positions from the signals.
Use an attractive potential to move the robot toward a banana.
Use repulsive potentials to avoid the poops.
Combine the attractive and repulsive contributions into a resultant force.
Use the direction of this force to control the robot.
Convert the desired motion into left and right wheel velocities.
Detect when a banana has been reached and select another one.
Stop when all bananas have been collected.
Important
The objective is to implement the potential-field algorithm. Do not manually define a path or waypoints. The robot trajectory should emerge from the attractive forces generated by the bananas and the repulsive forces generated by the poops.
